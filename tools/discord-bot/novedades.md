# 🛠️ Next update: what's new

## 🎨 Interface
- **Grades · "Stop at"**: the letters on the buttons are much bigger. Selected grades are now animated: a spinning rainbow on ∞, X and Z+, and a pulsing border on the rarest ones.
- **Backpack · Accessories**: they're now sorted into categories: **Damage**, **Luck**, **Coins** and **Gems**.
- **Backpack · Armor**: with "Hide armor" on, the character preview in your backpack no longer shows it either.

## ⚔️ Gameplay
- **Auto**: if you leave your plot and come back, it turns itself on again.
- **Helmets**: you no longer lose your avatar's head if it's a big hat.
- **Armor**: the 4th stat of a piece can no longer be Max Health.

Thanks for playing! 💜
